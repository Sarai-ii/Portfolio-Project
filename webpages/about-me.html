Hi, I'm Sarai!

Born and raised in New York City, I also spent part of my college years in Atlanta, Georgia — giving me a unique blend of urban energy and Southern warmth. My upbringing was a mix of city hustle and rural calm, visiting my great-grandmother in the brisk mountains of West Virginia, where I learned to appreciate the simple things in life. These experiences taught me how to balance fast-paced ambition without losing my grounding, to stay curious, and to always appreciate the small things in life.

My professional journey reflects this balance: I’m always learning, adapting to new technologies, and finding ways to make meaningful, people-centered impact. Whether I’m building an interactive web experience or experimenting with AI tools, I stay true to what matters most to me.

    My Core Values:

  • Integrity in everything I create
  • Growth through lifelong learning
  • Creativity fueled by curiosity
  • Community through collaboration
  • Empathy in both leadership and design
